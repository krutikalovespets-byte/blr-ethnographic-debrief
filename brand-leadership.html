Section 04
If leadership takes 3 decisions from this research, these are the ones.
Infrastructure, go-to-market, and strategy. All grounded in transcript evidence. Click to expand evidence quotes.
01
Launch 30-minute pharmacy in BLR's top-5 pet-parent hubs.
Pharmacy urgency is the highest-loyalty, highest-margin, non-price-sensitive moment on the platform. One failed delivery costs 6 months of category trust. Winning this moment creates a moat qcomm cannot replicate — they can do speed, but they cannot do vet-recommended dispensing at 10pm.
Operations + Product Infrastructure investment This quarter
▼ Expand evidence
Transcript Evidence
"I ordered at 10pm. The delivery never came. I had to manage my dog through the night without the medication." — Lakshmi, Respondent
"When my pet is sick, I want it now. Not tomorrow morning. Now." — Nazia, Respondent
5 of 17 respondents described pharmacy urgency as a primary pain point. None described a satisfactory resolution from the platform.
02
Fix the Ashok acquisition problem.
5 of 17 respondents are Ashok-type — the highest-LTV, most protocol-driven, most efficient segment. 4 of 5 are not Supertails users. They have existing systems. Supertails is not in them. Every month this continues is a cohort of high-LTV customers being acquired by competitors by default.

"My vet gives me a list. I buy from wherever it's in stock and ships fast. I don't have brand loyalty to a platform." — Nitesh J, non-ST Ashok respondent
Komal K and Reshma Q: both established routines with zero Supertails presence. Neither had been targeted or acquired by any Supertails channel.
03
Build the conviction parent into the brand strategy.
The manifesto has 4 personas. Bangalore has a 5th — the conviction-driven, welfare-first, high-knowledge parent who doesn't fit any archetype. This is Smrithi, Lakshmi, Unit F, Vihaar, Unit N. 5 of 17. They are the highest advocacy multiplier in the room and currently underserved by both content and positioning.

"I don't want to be told to love my pet. I already do. I want to be given the right tools." — Vihaar, conviction parent
Smrithi and Lakshmi: both described the brand as warm but insufficient — the depth of their knowledge exceeded the depth of the content they were being served.
Two of three decisions are executable this quarter with existing teams. Decision 3 is strategy — it shapes the AOP, not a sprint ticket.
