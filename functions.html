Merch on Platform

Nobody browses for accessories.
They impulse-buy them.

Accessories are never the primary search. They surface at the moment of first ownership (the "buy everything" phase), at vet visits (post-worry relief purchases), and at birthdays. The category is driven by triggers — not by catalogue browsing. Small gifts in every order are remembered years later.

Digital · Paid Team

They find you when they're scared.
Not when they're browsing.

Supertails is discovered via Google search for medicine and symptoms — not brand campaigns. The intent signal is fear, not desire. Reorders go to whoever sends the right push notification at the right moment. Social content influences breed decisions 6–12 months upstream of any purchase. You're not in that conversation yet.

Brand Team

The sidekick isn't a product.
It's a person who shows up when they're scared.

10 of 17 respondents are immigrants to Bangalore. Every single one described the moment they needed pet-care knowledge and had no one to call. The brand that structurally fills this gap wins acquisition, retention, and advocacy simultaneously. Indie vs Purebred isn't a budget signal — it's a values signal that demands two completely different brand voices.
