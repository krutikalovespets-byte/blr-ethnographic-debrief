6 findings every Supertails employee should know
Acquisition

They find us when they're scared, not when they're browsing. Google search for medicine or symptoms is the #1 acquisition path — not brand campaigns.

"I found Supertails on Google when I was searching for medicine for Rio."
— Ritik (A) · ST user
Retention

The reorder goes to whoever is cheapest at that exact moment. 6 of 17 actively price-check Zepto before reordering food from Supertails. The pharmacy is the only defensible moat.

"Royal Canin on Zepto is cheaper now. I check before I order."
— Ritik (A) · ST user
Healthcare

Vet loyalty is non-transferable. 11 of 17 named a specific vet they trust and said they'd resist switching. The first vet visit defines everything — forever.

"I would like to continue with him rather than anyone else. You understand when a doctor is good."
— Smrithi (D) · ST user
Brand

10 of 17 are immigrants. Every single one described the moment they needed pet-care knowledge and had nobody to call. The brand that fills this structurally wins acquisition and advocacy.

"When I came to Bangalore with two dogs, I had no idea where anything was."
— Lakshmi · ST user
Product

A software engineer filed a proper bug report — screenshots, steps, expected vs actual. He got zero response and is now a Zepto food buyer. App performance is a commercial problem, not a tech one.

"I already sent feedback mail but I didn't receive anything back."
— Ritik (A) · ST user · Software Engineer
Persona Gap

The manifesto has 4 archetypes. BLR has a 5th: conviction-driven, welfare-first, high-knowledge parents who don't fit any existing model. 5 of 17. Highest advocacy multiplier in the room.

"We feed about 40 street animals. It is not separate from our life."
— Unit F · ST user
